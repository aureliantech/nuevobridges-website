Two different courts

Immigration court decides status. Federal court decides whether the detention is legal.

Call 575-000-0000
  1. 01

    Immigration court

    Decides whether someone can stay in the country. It can take months or years.

  2. 02

    Federal court

    A separate lawsuit, called a habeas petition. It asks one question: is holding this person lawful right now?

  3. 03

    Why it matters

    A federal judge can order someone released while the immigration case is still going.

People get transferred. Often without warning.

ICE can move someone to a facility in another state. A transfer can change which court hears the case and can cost time. The sooner we know where they are, the sooner we can act.
