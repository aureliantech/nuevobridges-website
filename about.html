Background

Two decades before immigration law.

Gina practised corporate, real estate and environmental law, advising developers, construction firms, hospitality companies and energy investors. She owned and ran an environmental consulting firm.

She has represented sovereign Tribal Nations on questions of sovereignty and land, and Native American individuals in tribal courts. She is admitted in the Nambe Pueblo and Santa Clara Pueblo tribal courts.

That is unusual background for an immigration lawyer, and it is why the employer-side work, the tribal and cross-border work, and the highway cases sit in one practice.
