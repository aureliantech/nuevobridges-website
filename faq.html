H-2A · Farm employers

Seasonal labor and harvest timing

Most farm owners start with a labor problem, not with the words “H-2A.” Call and describe the season you are trying to staff. We will tell you whether the program fits and what it would involve.

Lead time is the biggest variable in H-2A. Certification and visa processing both take weeks, not days, and a late start is the most common way employers end up short-handed at exactly the wrong moment. Sixty to ninety days out gives the most room. Calling later does not rule anything out; it narrows the options.

They are not the same fight. Immigration court decides someone’s status and whether they can be removed. Federal habeas is a separate case in federal district court that asks a judge one question: is holding this person lawful right now. Both can be going at once.

Truck accidents

After a serious truck accident

It depends on who is involved, not just where the crash happened. The parties’ citizenship, the carrier’s operating authority, and how the claims are structured can all push a case into federal court or keep it in state court. Most people cannot tell from the outside, and that is what the first call is for.

Get medical help first. If you can, note the trucking company, the location and the date. Call before you give a recorded statement or accept a quick offer from an insurer.

The first call is free. If we can help, Gina’s consultation is paid and you see the price before you book it. If she takes your case, there is no attorney fee unless we recover.
