H-2A · Farm employers

Seasonal labor and harvest timing

Many farm owners start with labor need, not “H-2A.” If lawful foreign seasonal labor may fit, call — we’ll talk through your farm and timing and say whether H-2A is the path.

Lead time is the biggest variable in H-2A timing — certification and visa processing both take weeks, not days, and a late start is the most common way employers end up short-handed at exactly the wrong moment. If you already know your season's labor need, calling 60–90 days out gives the most room to work with; calling later doesn't rule anything out, it just narrows the options. Either way, call and we'll map your specific calendar.

ICE detention · Federal habeas

When someone you know is detained

Yes. Share what you know — facility, dates, your relationship. We’ll explain possible next steps and whether we can help.

They're not the same fight. Immigration court decides someone's immigration status and whether they can be removed from the country. Federal habeas is a separate case, filed in federal district court, that asks a judge to review whether the detention itself is lawful right now — regardless of how the immigration case turns out. You can be dealing with both at once. On the first call, we'll help you understand which one applies to what you're facing, or whether both do.

Federal trucking accidents

After a serious truck accident

It depends on who's involved, not just where the crash happened. Diversity of citizenship between the parties, the carrier's operating authority, and how the claims are structured can all push a trucking case into federal court — or keep it in state court. Most people can't tell which applies from the outside, and that's fine; it's what the first call is for.

Get medical help first. If you can, note the trucking company, location, and date. Call before you give a recorded statement or accept a quick insurance offer — we’ll tell you what to watch for.
