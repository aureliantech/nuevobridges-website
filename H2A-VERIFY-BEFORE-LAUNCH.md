The rebuilt H-2A page now states program facts rather than atmosphere. That's
what makes it useful, and it's also what puts it on her bar licence. Every
factual claim on the page is listed here with its source so she can confirm or
correct it in one pass.

## Deliberately not on the page

- **No wage figures.** The AEWR methodology has been through the 2023 rule's
  vacatur, an injunction covering 17 states that has produced two separate
  effective dates for every update since, and an October 2025 interim final
  rule adopting an OEWS-based methodology, with a challenge to DOL's
  methodology still pending. Any number printed here goes stale fast. The page
  says so explicitly and routes the question to the call.
- **No fee figures.** The page says fees are quoted on the consultation.
- **No claim that the firm handles emergency filings, audits, WHD
  investigations, association or joint-employer filings, or worker
  recruitment.** Nobody has told me whether she does. If she does, each is
  worth its own line — the emergency-filing one especially, since it is the
  highest-intent traffic on the page.
