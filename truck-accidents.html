Why these are different

A truck case is not a car case.

Call 575-205-4084
  1. 01

    More than one company can be responsible

    The driver. The carrier. The company that loaded the trailer. The company that maintained the brakes.

  2. 02

    Federal rules apply

    Interstate trucking runs on federal safety regulations — hours behind the wheel, inspections, maintenance records. A car crash has none of that.

  3. 03

    The other side is bigger

    Commercial carriers have insurers and defense counsel on call. They are not waiting to see how you feel next month.

The proof is on the truck. It does not stay there.

Trucks and trucking companies keep records of a trip, and a truck does not stay parked. Records are easier to ask for early than late. The sooner we know about the crash, the more there is to work with.
